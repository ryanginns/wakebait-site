Common questions

My alarm didn't go off. WakeBait uses Apple's alarm system, so make sure you allowed alarm permission when the app first asked. You can check this in Settings › WakeBait.

How do I add a sound? Tap Add sound, choose any video from your camera roll, and trim it to the part you want. The audio is extracted on your phone and never leaves it.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose WakeBait, and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore a purchase? Tap Restore Purchases on the WakeBait Pro screen while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
