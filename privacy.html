Privacy Policy

Wakebait for iOS · Last updated 28 September 2026

Wakebait is made by Ryan Ginns in Lennox Head, Australia. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

What we collect

Wakebait does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your videos and sounds

When you add a sound, you choose a video using Apple's system photo picker. Wakebait extracts the audio on your device and stores it in the app's private storage on your phone. Your videos and sounds are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Wakebait Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. To manage subscriptions we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase and subscription status from Apple. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Alarms and Live Activities

Alarms run through Apple's AlarmKit and appear on your Lock Screen. This all happens on your device.

Support email

If you email us for help, we'll use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

Wakebait is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
